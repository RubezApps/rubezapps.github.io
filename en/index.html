01 / FEATURESFor parents & caregivers

Little Days Parents

Baby tracker & parents community

One place for everyday baby care, for the whole family, and for the questions only other parents understand.

For adult parents and caregivers. Community answers come from other parents, not doctors; Little Days Parents does not diagnose, treat or replace medical advice.

01

Follow the little routines

Bottles, breastfeeding, food, diapers and sleep in one place, with a sleep timer and daily summaries.

02

Bring the family along

Invite up to 4 people into your family. Everyone signs in with their own account, the journal syncs across phones, and you choose who can view or edit.

03

Ask other parents

Post questions, get answers and comments, and share photos and short videos of your baby’s moments.

04

Feeding reminders & alerts

Feeding reminders and sleep-timer notifications on your phone, depending on your plan.
