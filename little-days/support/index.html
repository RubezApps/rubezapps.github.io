How can we help?

A clear route to a person, whenever you need help with Little Days.

Updated 29 October 2026

Contact Little Days support

For account help, privacy questions, deletion requests or a problem using the app, email our support team.

appmakernguyen@gmail.com

Open email app ↗

Sending email opens your email provider; this website does not send a support message automatically.

Help us understand the problem

  • Describe what happened and what you expected.
  • Include your account email if the issue concerns your account.
  • For a technical issue, include your Android version and app version if known.
  • Only include a screenshot after hiding baby details, email addresses and other private content.

Never send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or unnecessary baby health records.

Report community content

Use the post or comment’s options in the app to report content or block an account. If you cannot access the app, email us with enough non-sensitive information to locate the content. A report is reviewed by the operator and does not guarantee removal.

This mailbox is not an emergency or medical service. For urgent danger, use local emergency services.
