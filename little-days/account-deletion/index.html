Delete your account

Leave Little Days and request removal of your account and associated app data.

Updated 29 October 2026

On this pageDelete in the appRequest by emailWhat is deletedAfter your request

1Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to Little Days.
  2. Open your Baby’s profile from General by tapping the baby photo or name.
  3. Find Privacy & support → Delete app account and data.
  4. Read the family warning, type DELETE, and confirm.

You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion: use the email option below if you cannot sign in.

2Request deletion by email

Send a request to appmakernguyen@gmail.com, preferably from the email used for your Little Days account. Include that account email and say you want your Little Days account and associated data deleted.

This prepares an email on your device. You must send it to submit your request. Nothing is submitted or deleted by filling this form.

If no email app opens, send an email manually with subject Little Days account deletion. Do not send your password, verification codes, baby photos or identification documents.

What is deleted?

Your app sign-in account and associated active app records: community profile, posts, photos and videos, comments, likes, app support records, notification/device records, plan/reward records and related reports or blocks.

Family owner: your shared family’s baby profile, journal, memberships and invitations are also deleted for all members. Other members’ separate sign-in accounts remain. Save anything you need first.

Invited member: your membership and journal entries you authored are deleted; the rest of the family’s records remain.

Copies saved by others, provider security/operational logs, independent Google payment/advertising records and any provider recovery copies are not remotely erased by this action. See the retention explanation. Uninstalling the app is not account deletion. Account deletion does not cancel a store subscription.

What happens after an email request?

Requests go to our support mailbox and are handled manually. We verify ownership before removing account data; a request does not delete another person’s account just because their email is entered. We aim to respond within 30 days and will tell you if further verification or applicable legal requirements mean more time is needed.

You can also ask to remove particular content or correct information without deleting your whole account. Describe what you want removed in your email. You may request removal of the correspondence when it is no longer needed.
