Terms & Community Rules

The community agreement used in Little Days Parents.

Updated 13 November 2026

Little Days Parents is for adult parents and caregivers. You must be 18 or older to create an account or take part in the community. You must have authority to store and share the baby's information and permission to post photos of other people.

Questions and answers in the community come from other parents. They are not medical advice. For urgent concerns about a baby's health, contact a doctor or emergency services.

Keep the community safe

No child exploitation or abuse, sexual images, nudity, harassment, threats, hate, scams, illegal content, impersonation, spam or sharing another person's private information. Do not post intimate baby photos. Do not offer dangerous medical instructions or present the journal as a replacement for professional care.

Child safety

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and for any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children. Such content is removed, the account responsible is suspended or closed, and we report it to the relevant authorities as required by law. Do not post photos of babies or children that are nude, partly dressed, bathing or in intimate situations, and do not share details that could locate a child, such as an address or school.

To report a child-safety concern, use “Report” on the post or comment in the app, or email appmakernguyen@gmail.com with the subject “Child safety”. Our child-safety contact is Vũ Trần Nhật Nguyên (RubezApps). If a child is in immediate danger, contact local police or emergency services first.

Your posts

Post only material you have permission to share. You retain ownership and permit us to store and display it to provide the app. Community posts are visible to all signed-in app users. You can delete your own posts. We may remove content or suspend community activity when rules are broken.

Reporting and moderation

Use report and block controls on posts and comments. Reports go to the operator for review; reporting does not guarantee removal. The operator must review reports and take appropriate action. Support is available in Profile → Privacy & support.

Plans

Little Days Parents has a Free plan and two paid options with the same features: Plus (paid monthly) and Pro (paid yearly). The features of each plan are shown in the app. Plus and Pro have no ads. A family on the Free plan includes 2 people (the owner and 1 more); a monthly family add-on allows up to 4 people. Plus and Pro include up to 4 people. A family can have at most 4 people. When paid plans are available in the app, Plus, Pro and extra family seats are auto-renewing subscriptions sold through Google Play on Android or the App Store on iPhone. Before you buy, the store shows the price, billing period and renewal terms. The store takes the payment; we never see your card details. A subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in your Google Play or Apple account subscription settings, and cancelling stops the next renewal. Refunds follow the store’s rules. Deleting your app account does not cancel a subscription. On the Free plan, optional rewarded ads can give a small extra allowance.
