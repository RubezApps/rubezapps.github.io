Terms & Community Rules

The community agreement used in Little Days Parents.

Updated 8 November 2026

Little Days Parents is for adult parents and caregivers. You must have authority to store and share the baby's information and permission to post photos of other people.

Questions and answers in the community come from other parents. They are not medical advice. For urgent concerns about a baby's health, contact a doctor or emergency services.

Keep the community safe

No child exploitation or abuse, sexual images, nudity, harassment, threats, hate, scams, illegal content, impersonation, spam or sharing another person's private information. Do not post intimate baby photos. Do not offer dangerous medical instructions or present the journal as a replacement for professional care.

Your posts

Post only material you have permission to share. You retain ownership and permit us to store and display it to provide the app. Community posts are visible to all signed-in app users. You can delete your own posts. We may remove content or suspend community activity when rules are broken.

Reporting and moderation

Use report and block controls on posts and comments. Reports go to the operator for review; reporting does not guarantee removal. The operator must review reports and take appropriate action. Support is available in Profile → Privacy & support.

Plans

Little Days Parents has a Free plan and two paid options with the same features: Plus (paid monthly) and Pro (paid yearly). The features of each plan are shown in the app. Plus and Pro have no ads. A family on the Free plan includes 2 people (the owner and 1 more); a monthly family add-on allows up to 4 people. Plus and Pro include up to 4 people. A family can have at most 4 people. Current paid test mode takes no payment. Real subscriptions and ad rewards require their services to be connected. When enabled, subscriptions will show their price, renewal terms and cancellation route before purchase.

Notifications

Post notifications in the app (likes, comments and family posts) are cleared automatically after 24 hours. You can also clear them yourself at any time.

Care and availability

Logs are entered by caregivers and may contain errors. This is not medical advice, diagnosis or an emergency service. Keep a separate copy of important records. These preview terms will be reviewed with the final operator and production services before launch.
